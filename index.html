Bio

I'm currently a third-year PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Duke University, advised by Prof. Neil Gong. I received my Bachelor's degree from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2024.

My research interests lie in LLM agents and AI security, with a particular focus on the following area:

  • Web Agent Security: Investigating prompt injection attacks and defenses for web agents, and securing web agents via security policies.

I am looking for research internship opportunities for Summer 2027 — feel free to reach out at xilong.wang@duke.edu.
